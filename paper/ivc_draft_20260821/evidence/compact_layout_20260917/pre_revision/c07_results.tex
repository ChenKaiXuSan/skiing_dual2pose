\subsection{Matched occlusion and calibration sensitivity}
\label{subsec:matched_geometry}

We compare pose-only fusion with geometry-assisted reconstruction using predictions from the same corrupted images. One SAM3D call supplies both the original-image 2D keypoints and the 3D pose of the same selected person. These 2D observations are model-projected estimates, not ground truth or outputs of an independent detector. Unity projections locate the occluding patches only. The matched study covers clean inputs and random masks at ratios 0.5 and 1.0 on the left, right, or both views. All five methods retain the same 64,440 windows, batch size 256, sample order, and common-13 scoring after all-15 canonicalization. Learned weights are frozen. This seven-condition study uses fresh paired predictions and a different reported joint subset from the preceding all-15, 18-condition study; their numbers are not interchangeable. Complete protocols and metrics are in Supplementary Section~\ref{S-sec:c07_matched_geometry}.

With unperturbed supplied calibration, DLT-residual has the lowest MPJPE in six of seven conditions. Its clean error is 0.0988 versus 0.1553 for CanonFuse3D. Under the three ratio-0.5 masks, its MPJPE is 25.6--28.8\% lower, whereas CanonFuse3D has 1.8--9.5\% lower PA-MPJPE and 43.7--46.0\% lower acceleration error. Thus, the additional-input method improves position accuracy, while the pose-only method retains advantages on aligned configuration and temporal second-difference error in these conditions. CanonFuse3D also improves MPJPE over canonical averaging by 20.1--32.5\% under ratio-0.5 masks. At ratio 1.0 this advantage reverses for either single-side or bilateral masking, by 10.7--28.6\%. The results do not support automatic rejection of an arbitrarily degraded input view.

We independently perturb the supplied right-camera extrinsics by $0^\circ$, $1^\circ$, and $3^\circ$, retaining the left camera and the right-camera center. DLT-residual still has lower MPJPE than CanonFuse3D on clean and ratio-0.5 inputs at $1^\circ$. At $3^\circ$, CanonFuse3D has the lowest MPJPE and PA-MPJPE among the five methods on those four input conditions, but canonical averaging is best on both metrics in all three ratio-1.0 conditions. Pose-only scores are invariant by construction because neither their images nor their 3D inputs change. This experiment measures sensitivity to supplied calibration, not to physically moving a camera or altering SAM3D's internal camera estimates. Individual conditions need not deteriorate monotonically with angle.

Missing observations remain in all denominators. At bilateral ratio 1.0, at least one view fails in 54.85\% of pair--frame positions. After the unchanged batch-referenced transformation, 33.30\% of CanonFuse3D and 70.05\% of DLT/gated-DLT frames have collapsed common-13 configurations. A missing reference frame can affect an entire batch, rather than only the failed frame. These results characterize the coupled front-end, fallback, normalization, and prediction pipeline; they do not isolate failure of the fusion network or triangulation alone. Small acceleration error for a collapsed output is not evidence of successful motion recovery.
